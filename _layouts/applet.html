It reuses the chrome out of engine/shell.css (.shell-skip, .shell-bar, .shell-foot, .theme-toggle--bar) instead of restyling it here, so the bar and the footer cannot drift between a lab and an applet and the 1076px content row documented in HANDOFF.md holds on both. engine/applet/applet.css adds the --ap-* layer on top. What differs from a lab: no notation key (an applet has no pseudocode), no spec fetch, and the disclosure footer speaks about an applet rather than a puzzle. The page body is the applet itself, written in the page file. Front matter a page supplies: title shown in and as the h1 the page writes itself robots false on every applet, as on every lab scripts optional list of classic script paths, for a vendored library.
