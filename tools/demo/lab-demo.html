How this lab was built, and what I expect from you

How this lab was built

Following the Leiden Declaration on Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics, I want to be transparent that I built the puzzle engine, the concept maps, the applets, the build pipeline and the first drafts of the writing with the help of a large language model. I've read and checked most of what it produced, the mathematical claims, the hints and the wrong-answer messages, but there are almost certainly some small errors still in here. I take the responsibility for any such error, and I would like to hear about them. If something on one of these pages contradicts what you've worked out carefully, trust your own reasoning and tell me.

Nothing on this page calls an AI service. Your work is checked by a small interpreter running in your browser, your progress is saved on your own machine, and no request leaves the page.

Using AI on this lab

You could paste any of these puzzles into a chatbot and have the answer in seconds. The answer isn't what the lab is for. The blocks are written for you already, so the work left is deciding what order they go in and why, and that is the part that transfers to an algorithm you haven't seen before.

Use whatever helps you understand something you're stuck on. Don't use it to skip the reasoning.
