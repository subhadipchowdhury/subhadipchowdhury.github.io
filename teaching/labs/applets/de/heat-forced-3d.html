Forced heat equation

A rod held at a fixed temperature at each end, with a heat source along its length that never changes. Whatever it starts at, it settles into one shape, and that shape is decided by the source and the two end values together. The surface below is the whole history: position across, time back, temperature up.

\[ u_t = u_{xx} + f(x), \qquad x \in [0,1], \qquad u(0,t) = a, \qquad u(1,t) = b \]

Ready.

Drag to turn the surface. The ridge running back in time is the steady state; the ripples near the front are the starting shape being forgotten. Try a starting shape that disagrees with an end value and watch how fast the disagreement is erased.
