A teaching specification states the design choices behind your course material precisely enough that an AI model can follow them. Most of what an instructor knows about teaching a particular course is never written down; writing it down makes it reviewable, transmissible, and usable at scale.

- **"Teaching Your AI How You Teach"** - My column in [Teaching Matters](https://teaching.uchicago.edu/resources/teaching-matters), the Chicago Center for Teaching and Learning newsletter. Forthcoming; the direct link will be added here once it is published.
- **[How-to guide](/pedagogy/specification/how-to-guide.html)** - Ten steps, from narrowing the scope to putting the review on a schedule, with the prompts to paste at each stage. Assumes no prior experience with these tools.
- **[Additional resources](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1ubk9mRdUFx_3cR9EQhBMqXTJmLsaRcmy?usp=sharing)** - Public versions of two course specifications, with demonstration worksheets generated with and without them. Gated behind a UChicago login for now.
